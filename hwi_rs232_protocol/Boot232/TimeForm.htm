Time Formatting

  • Time formats are used for setting the internal processor clock
  • When setting the system time, 24 hour format must be used
  • HH:MM:SS
       
    HH = Hours    
    MM = Minutes  
    SS = Seconds

  • Examples

4:23 AM = 04:23:00

3:15 PM = 15:15:00
